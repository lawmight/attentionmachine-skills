# Attention Machine

Feels like a confident friend holding up proof as they talk, in the brand's colours. Calm cutting, real proof on screen, heavy type and a clear brand palette.

## General

### Colours


| Name      | Hex       | Job                                                                                          |
| --------- | --------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Primary   | `#0731F8` | The main colour: the block behind every headline                                             |
| Secondary | `#FED347` | The payoff: the last big word of a stack, or the number the story builds to                  |
| Cream     | `#F5F1EA` | Background behind dark icons, text and motion graphics                                       |
| Dark      | `#050505` | Background behind light icons, text and motion graphics                                      |
| White     | `#FFFFFF` | Captions and small text over footage, with a faint soft shadow; headline text on blue blocks |


- Overlays and clips keep their own colours.
- **Blue blocks:** every Unbounded headline sits on a solid primary block hugging the text: padding about 10% of the text size top and bottom and 35% each side, corners about 18%. One block per line, about 10% of the text size apart. No shadow on the text.



### Fonts

- **Headline:** Unbounded Bold. Hook, stacks, numbers, headlines.
- **Text:** Poppins Bold. Captions.

Both are free on Google Fonts.

Unbounded is very wide. Size it to fit the space it has (the safe zone in vertical), not to a fixed number.

### Headlines

- **Word stack:** the core idea in 2 or 3 stacked words, appearing as they're spoken: big, small, big. Big words are Unbounded Bold, lowercase, white on a blue block, the last one yellow. The small word is Poppins Bold, under half the size, white, no block. Two stacks back to back make the contrast ("better videos / same tools").
- **Big number:** a money figure or count in Unbounded Bold on a blue block, while it's said.

### Overlays

Whenever a company, app, place, product or scene is named, show it: a photo, or the brand's logo or app icon. Screen shares, clips and explainers go on a split instead.

- Hard edges, no border, no shadow.
- Several in a row for a list, one per item, changing on the spoken word.
- Place them where they read best: inside the safe zone in vertical wherever possible, even if that covers the face.

### Layouts

Pick by what he's doing:

- **Full screen**: he explains an idea or gives an opinion and there's nothing to show. Also the intro and the outro.
- **Screen share**: the screen with him beside it, whenever a screen recording has something to show. A split in vertical, picture-in-picture in horizontal.
- **Zoom into the screen** (not a punch-in on him): when he points at or names something on it, like a button, a price or a command. Up to 3x. It goes in and out in about 0.5 s, soft at both ends. Hold it while he talks about it, then glide straight to the next thing without zooming out in between.
- **Split**: screenshots, clips, explainers and lists he counts off (see splits).
- Switch layouts on a cut.
- Never hide him to make room. The screen without him only when the user asks.



### Splits

For anything he explains that isn't a single overlay: a screen share or screenshot, a video clip, a motion graphic or explainer. Also when he points or says "this" about something to show.

- As few layers as possible. Screenshots, screen recordings and clips fill their part edge to edge, cropped to fill, even past the safe zone. No frame, card or background around something that already has edges. Keep what he's talking about in view.
- Only things without edges of their own (icons, text, motion graphics) get a background: cream or dark, whichever stands apart from them, filling the part.
- The other part is the speaker at their normal size, never zoomed in. Look at the footage to frame him for each split.
- Starts and ends on a cut. Holds as long as he talks about what's shown, often ten seconds: the shown part switches to the next thing on the spoken word, and the split ends with that topic. Things named during a split go in the shown part, not over their face.
- A clip from someone else's video freezes on its moment before it cuts to anything off-topic (a presenter, a transition).



### Pacing and cuts

- Tight jump cuts, no full stops: the words almost touch across a join (the next word starts about 30 ms after the last one dies away, over its fade).
- At every cut, the next line is heard about 160 ms before its clip shows (200 ms in horizontal videos); the old clip stays on screen under it.
- Zoom (a punch-in, about 118%, or 115% in horizontal videos) only for two reasons: to hide a jump cut (takes alternate normal and punch-in), or to make a point on a key line. Never just to keep things moving. One framing per take otherwise.
- Splits never punch in on him. Zooming into the screen in the shown part is fine.



### Motion

- Captions and splits cut in and out.
- Everything else arrives with a small, quick entrance (a pop, a settle) and acts out the idea: lists build up item by item on the spoken word, what gets cut leaves, before turns into after. Never slow or floaty.
- Headlines land with a snap and then hold still while they're read. Stack words arrive one by one.



### Sound

- No music, unless explicitly asked.
- Sound effects: yes, very subtle, felt more than heard: 20 dB under the voice (about 10%), or 10 dB in horizontal videos. A soft pop when an overlay or stack word appears, a soft click for the next overlay in a run or the shown part of a split changing, a soft swish when a split opens, a soft whoosh for flying logos. Taken from the user's sound library or the skill's own sounds, made with ElevenLabs only when one is missing (see the motion guide).



## Vertical videos (Reels, Shorts, TikTok)

Sizes are for a 1080x1920 frame.

### Captions (the whole video)

- Poppins Bold, white, about 46px, one line, at most about 22 characters.
- On a small dark pill hugging the text: dark at 15% opacity, padding about 40% of the text size each side, corners about 25%.
- Short phrases of two to five words, the whole phrase at once, in time with the voice.
- All lowercase, even at the start of a sentence; only names, brands and "I" keep a capital. Never any punctuation (no full stops or commas, but keep question and exclamation marks). Apostrophes stay: "don't", "it's", etc.
- Centred, **bottom**. Stays put the whole video, also under overlays.
- During a split screen, captions sit centred on the seam between the two halves.



### Text hook (first 3 to 5 seconds)

- Two lines, centred, **top**: a short lowercase lead-in in Poppins Bold (about 52px, white), saying what it is ("new Claude feature"), then the name in Unbounded Bold caps (about 76px, white on a blue block). No punctuation.
- Starts about 80px under the safe zone's top edge, never right on it. It may overlap the top of the head, never the eyes.
- Logos are welcome around the hook when the video is about a brand, kept clear of the text and their head. Nothing forced.
- Stays while the first sentences play, then cuts away.



### Headlines

- Stacks: big words about 96px, small words about 44px. Centred, **top**.
- Big numbers: about 120px. Centred, **top**.



### Overlays

- About 65% of the frame width.
- Shifted left, centre or right from one overlay to the next.
- The caption stays visible below.



### Layouts

- **Full screen** (most of the video): the speaker from the chest up.
- **Split** (50/50, stacked), also for screen shares:
  - Top half: the thing shown, filling it edge to edge (see splits).
  - Bottom half: the speaker, moved up or down so the top of their head sits about 5% of the half's height (about 50px) under the seam. Find the top of the head (hair, cap or hat included) for each split.
  - Captions sit centred on the seam.
- **Behind**, when he talks about something with parts (a product reaching accounts, a hub and spokes): a motion graphic in the top half behind him, no split, him cut out in front (person masks).
- No insets or cutaways.



## Horizontal videos (YouTube, 16:9)

Sizes are for a 1920x1080 frame; scale them with the width.

- No hook and no captions. The viewer chose the video and watches with sound.
- Anything technical on screen gets a line in plain words under it ("Remotion" / "makes the graphics").
- Titles on cards and panels are white on dark and dark on cream, without a primary block. Their plain-words lines are the same colour at about 60% opacity.
- The dark background has a barely visible lift in the middle and a still grain, baked into one picture: a live gradient over 4K footage can run a cloud export out of memory.



### Type


| Use                               | Font                  | Size                                                    |
| --------------------------------- | --------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------- |
| Section card title                | Unbounded Bold        | 92px                                                    |
| H1: the title of a panel or scene | Unbounded Bold        | 82px                                                    |
| H2: a point in a list, a term     | Unbounded Bold        | 50px                                                    |
| Plain-words line                  | Poppins Medium        | 44px under a section card title, 34px under an H1 or H2 |
| Number in a list                  | Poppins Bold          | 26px                                                    |
| Code                              | JetBrains Mono Medium | 30px                                                    |




### Layouts

- **Full screen**: a jump cut on him is never bare. Cover it with what he names, a card or the screen recording. If nothing fits, crop in to about 115% on every other clip, eye line held. A section card resets the crop.
- **Picture-in-picture**, for screen shares:
  - The screen recording fills the frame: no background, padding, rounded corners or shadow.
  - He's a small portrait (9:16) window, about 16% of the frame width, framed head and shoulders, corners about 8%, a soft shadow. Never zoomed in.
  - Two places only, about 5% of the frame from the edge: **right, centred** top to bottom (the default), or **left, centred**. He moves left only when he'd cover what's being shown, and moves on a cut.
  - When zooming in, centre the target in the part of the frame he leaves free, with a margin from their picture.
- **Split**: side by side, the thing shown on the left, him on the right half.
- **Split list**, for a list he counts off ("two problems", "three steps"): a panel on the dark background takes the left half, sliding in from the left as one piece and pushing him to the right half, then sliding back out the same way. An H1 at the top left, then each point as an H2 with its plain-words line, sliding in a short way on its line. Numbers sit in small primary circles.
- **Explainer**, for what a tool does: the full frame on cream, their small picture on the right. What goes in on the left, the tool's real icon in the middle, what comes out on the right, made from the real thing (their own voice as the waveform, their own words coming out). An H1 at the top left with its plain-words line ("Speech to text" / "sound in, words out"). About 20 seconds. It cuts on. When the next clip is him full screen, their picture grows to full screen over it; otherwise it cuts.
- **Behind**, when he shows results: up to 2 pictures or muted clips slide in behind him from either side, slightly tilted, with a white border and a soft shadow, and slide out together.



### Graphics

- **Section card**: every new section or step. A dark screen with one centred title, and the plain-words line under it when the title is a tool or a technical term. Nothing above the title. It cuts on with the line that opens the section, stays at least 2 seconds, and swipes off downwards while the video and their voice carry on.
- **App icons**: when he names apps, their real icons, never name labels. Each slides in on its word from its own side, and they slide out together. Left and right mirror each other around the centre. Four apps take the four corners in the order he names them. One or two sit one on each side, about twice the size.
- **Term line**: for a term or a number the audience won't know ("AI agent", "trial reels"). The term as an H2 with its plain-words line under it, dark on a white pill with a thick primary border, centred low in the frame whatever the layout. It slides up on the word and back down after its line, at least 3 seconds.



### Motion

- Things cut on or slide in and out instead of popping, about 0.35 s, eased. The split list's push takes about 0.5 s. A point in a list may fade while it slides.
- No tilt, rotation or bounce, apart from the pictures behind him.



### Pacing and cuts

- Joins overlap by 350 ms, with no dip in volume. Pauses over 0.35 s come out.
- Asides and jokes go full screen.
- Waiting (on an AI, a page, an upload) is cut out, never sped up.



### Sound

- A thud when a section card cuts on, a swoosh when something slides in or out (peaking in the middle of its move), a pop when a point, a result or a term line lands, a click on real clicks, a key press on their real key presses, typing under code being typed, a ding or a deny when an explainer lets something through or stops it.
- No sound on zooms, on cuts between him and the screen, or per word.
