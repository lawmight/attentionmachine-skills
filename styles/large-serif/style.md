# Large Serif

Feels like a classy magazine list come to life. The speaker stands in a styled room; the words are quiet, the structure is loud.

Sizes are for a 1080×1920 frame.

## Colours

- White `#FFFFFF` for all text, with a soft dark shadow so it reads on light rooms.
- No other colours in text or graphics. Pictures keep their own colours.

## Fonts

- **Serif:** a classic book serif (Times, Libre Caslon, EB Garamond). Everything uses it: captions, numbers, titles.

All free on Google Fonts, except Times.

## Text types

### Caption (the whole video)

- Serif, white, about 76px, regular.
- One spoken word at a time, every word, in time with the voice. The next word replaces it on the spot.
- Centred, **off-center**: over the speaker's waist, below the hands.
- Lowercase, except names and brands (they keep their capitals). No punctuation.

### Step number

- Serif, white, all caps, about 72px: "NUMBER 1", "NUMBER 2"…
- Centred, **top**, a little air above it.
- Cuts in with a punch-in at the start of each step and stays 2–3 seconds.

### Step title (stack)

- The step's name in two to four stacked lines of mixed size: the important words big (about 140px), the small words between them small (about 60px). The last line can be all caps.
- Example shape: big word / small word / big word / small word.
- Lines sit tight, centred, built word by word as they're spoken. It replaces the caption while it's up.
- Centred, **off-center**. Leaves on the next cut, together with the step number.

## Title (first seconds)

- No separate title. The first sentence, one word at a time, is the hook.

## Pictures

- **Photo bursts:** three to five photos of the speaker's real life, back to back, about half a second each, when they talk about themselves or a result.
- Big: about 70% of the frame width, centred near the top, laid right over the speaker's head and chest. No border, no rounded corners, no shadow.
- The caption stays on top of the photos.
- Rare: once or twice per video.

## Layouts

- Speaker full screen, full body, wide. No splits, insets or cutaways.

## Pacing and cuts

- Pauses removed with jump cuts. Every cut swaps between the wide frame and a punch-in (about 115–120%).
- Each step starts on a punch-in with its number and title.
- Something changes every word, so the frame can stay still otherwise.

## Motion

- Captions, numbers, titles and photos cut in and cut out. No fades, no slides, no pops.
- The punch-in is a hard cut, not a slow zoom.

## Sound

- A soft hit or whoosh on each punch-in that starts a step, and a light click on photo bursts.
- Music, if any, is a soft bed far under the voice.

## Ending

- The last step leads straight into a summary sentence. No end card.

## Don't

- No coloured words, no highlights, no boxes behind text.
- No more than one word on screen, except the step number and title.
- No animated text, no transitions between clips.
